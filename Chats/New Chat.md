**First Step in Ethical Hacking**
=====================================

The first step in ethical hacking is to gain a thorough understanding of Open Source Intelligence (OSINT). This involves learning how to collect, analyze, and utilize publicly available information about a target system or organization.

According to the PNPT Certification Path (1. What need to Learn), OSINT is one of the core components of ethical hacking. By mastering OSINT skills, you'll be able to gather valuable insights about your target without compromising any security measures.
